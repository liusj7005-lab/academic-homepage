Xin Lai

Post-doctoral Fellow
Department of Electronic and Computer Engineering, HKUST

I am a post-doctoral fellow at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST). My research focuses on array signal processing, MIMO radar systems, sparse array design, and direction-of-arrival (DOA) estimation. I am particularly interested in coprime array configurations, synthetic coarray techniques, and their applications in radar and wireless communications.

Research Interests

Sparse Array Design
Designing novel sparse array configurations such as coprime arrays, nested arrays, and fragmented arrays to maximize degrees of freedom for DOA estimation.
MIMO Radar Signal Processing
Developing advanced signal processing techniques for MIMO radar systems, including synthetic coarray methods and joint DOD-DOA estimation algorithms.
DOA Estimation Algorithms
Research on high-resolution direction-of-arrival estimation methods, including MUSIC variants, tensor-based approaches, and optimization techniques.
See more research topics
Non-Circular Signal Processing
Exploiting the non-circularity property of communication signals for enhanced array processing and extended coarray design.
Bistatic Radar Systems
Signal processing techniques for bistatic radar configurations with coprime MIMO arrays and sum-difference coarray extensions.
Array Motion Strategies
Leveraging physical array motion to synthesize virtual arrays with extended aperture and improved estimation capabilities.
